Common Questions, Answered Honestly

Can I take Kidney Remedy while on dialysis?
Many of our customers use it alongside dialysis. Check with your care team before starting anything new, especially given dietary and mineral restrictions that can come with dialysis.
How long until I might notice something?
Customers who report changes often mention noticing something within 2–4 weeks. Any lab changes would only show up at your next scheduled blood draw — track those with your care team, not with how you feel.
Will my nephrologist approve of this?
We can't speak for your doctor. Bring them the ingredient list — it's food-based — and let your actual lab results, discussed with your care team, guide any decisions.
Is this a replacement for dialysis?
No. Kidney Remedy is not a replacement for dialysis or any medical treatment. Dialysis is a life-sustaining treatment. Only your medical team can advise on any change to your dialysis schedule, based on your actual lab results — never stop or reduce treatment on your own.
